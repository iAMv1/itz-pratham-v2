# LOGO BRIEF — Pratham Nahata

Brand essence: **"Built at the Bikaner gate, deployed everywhere."**
Full-stack × ML engineer — jharokha/jaali craft × shipped systems. Warm Rajasthani identity, engineering precision.

## What the mark must do
- Read as **one idea at 16px** (favicon) and hold up at 1200px (watermark/OG)
- Communicate: **threshold/building** (jharokha arch) + **engineering/systems** (lattice, network) + **energy** (saffron sun)
- Work in **1 color** (ink or paper), 2-color (saffron + ink), and full identity palette
- Work on cream paper AND near-black dark mode
- No clip-art, no gradients-heavy, no text-only wordmark as the primary mark

## Iteration A — "The Jharokha Gate"
- A bold **arched doorway** (the hero's jharokha motif) — threshold into the work
- Inside the arch: a **saffron sun disc** sitting on a **lattice grid** (2×2 bars = jali + code)
- The arch doubles as the letter **P / N** in negative space
- Palette: saffron block + ink arch + cream sun (or ink arch + saffron sun)
- Mood: heritage × precision. Favicon-clean, memorable, one idea

## Iteration B — "The Lattice Node"
- A **hexagonal jali cell** (bandhani/jaali geometry) with a **filled center node** — a graph node, a neuron, a system
- Surrounding hexes hinted by two partial arcs — the network extends beyond the mark
- Saffron node = the signal; ink lattice = the structure
- Mood: engineering-first — reads "GNNs, systems, networks" before "heritage"
- Strongest at medium sizes (nav, favicon); needs care at 16px

## Iteration C — "The Keystone Arrow"
- A minimal **jharokha arch** whose **keystone is an upward arrow** — "built here, shipping upward"
- The arch stroke doubles as a **bracket / terminal prompt** (`>_` whisper for devs)
- Saffron arrow, ink arch; single-line version possible for wordmark lockups
- Mood: builder energy, motion, "ships" — the most contemporary of the three

## Wordmark lockup (for all iterations)
- "PRATHAM NAHATA" in **Teko** (600–700), letterspaced, uppercase
- Optional Devanagari accent: **प्रथम नाहटा** in Yatra One as a secondary lockup

## Deliverables per iteration
1. SVG, 1-color + 2-color + full-palette variants
2. Favicon-safe 64px version (saffron block treatment for contrast)
3. Dark-mode variant (cream strokes on near-black)
4. 3 placements: nav 36px, favicon, footer/watermark

## Avoid
- Generic laptop/code-bracket/gear clichés
- 3D, gradients-as-crutch, drop shadows
- More than 3 shapes — the mark must survive 16px
